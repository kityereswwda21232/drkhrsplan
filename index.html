How to use this planner

1. Complete the daily blocks

Check off each study topic after active recall, not just after reading it.

2. Protect quiz preparation

Use the final block for a 20-question quiz and correction of every missed item.

3. Revisit weak topics

Mark difficult items and return to them during cumulative review days.

4. Update exam dates

When the official timetable is released, use the syllabus tracker notes or send it here to revise the plan.
